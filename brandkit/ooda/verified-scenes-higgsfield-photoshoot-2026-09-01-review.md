## Bounded recovery route

Do not ask a generative restyle model to preserve final garment or font-statue pixels. Use the successful candidates only as native lighting and composition references.

1. Lock the exact SKU and hero sources by SHA-256.
2. Generate or retain only the environment, camera, shadows, atmospheric depth, and non-product bodies.
3. Place the protected garment/model and hero layers through masks without regenerating their internal pixels.
4. Apply external light wrap, contact shadows, reflections, depth blur, grain, and color grade outside or around protected masks.
5. Produce a source-binding receipt, mask receipt, protected-pixel hash check, independent visual QA, and founder approval before promotion.

This is the viable path to combine Higgsfield-level scene integration with SkyyRose-level product truth.
