## Native integration gate

The candidate fails if any one of these is true:

1. A subject appears to sit on top of the environment instead of occupying its volume.
2. Feet, garment hems, hands, hair, or sculpture edges show halos, hard alpha contours, or incompatible sharpness.
3. Subjects disagree on lens perspective, camera height, horizon, key direction, fill ratio, rim behavior, color temperature, grain, or atmospheric depth.
4. Contact shadows do not originate at the physical contact point or fail to follow the same light geometry.
5. Wet-floor reflections do not align with the subject, sculpture, environment, and floor plane.
6. Garment graphics, construction, side panels, pocket geometry, patches, lettering, or colorways differ from exact SKU sources.
7. A collection logo or product-exclusive graphic is reused outside its canonical scope.
8. The scene passes source hashes but still looks optically composited at normal viewing size or 200 percent inspection.
