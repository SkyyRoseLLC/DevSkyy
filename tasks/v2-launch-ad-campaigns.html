Locked constraints

  • Brand name over tagline. "It's about SKYYROSE, not the tagline" (founder, 2026-07-12). No headline reads "Luxury grows from concrete." The house sheet shows the tagline — the monogram grows out of concrete — without printing it.
  • The Five only. Kith · Oaklandish · Culture Kings · Fear of God · Palm Angels. Their craft descriptors go in prompts; their names never do. European luxury-house lineage is locked out.
  • No blue. No navy. Anywhere. Every prompt carries the negative.
  • Each collection keeps its own canon. "Hurts is the bloodline that raised me" is Love Hurts only. Black Rose is armor. Signature is the blueprint. Kids Capsule is inheritance, no pastels, no cartoons.
  • Garment identity is pixels, not filenames. Every sheet with a garment embeds a verified Higgsfield Element built from a clean real photo or techflat. Hero-tier renders are QC'd side-by-side against that reference, detail-for-detail (bug-276), full-res only.
  • CTA follows product state. Published + in stock → "Shop". Pre-order → "Pre-order". Never a shop CTA on a pre-order SKU.
  • Muted-first. 70–85% of paid social plays silent. Captions burned in post (Archivo/Anton), center-safe band; every prompt leaves a natural tonally-calm area for them.
  • Gender-neutral. No "for him/her", no "men's/women's".
  • Objective = both (founder-locked 2026-07-16): a warm brand cut for retargeting and a 9–15s product-forward cold cut with the CTA in the first half.
  • One manifest → one y → one call. Founder-mandated 2026-07-18. No batch pre-approval. Every credit below is quoted from get_cost this session docs .

Visual system — fixed across the pack

One system, six hooks. Different doors, same house.

[VISUAL SYSTEM — applies across the pack]
Palette: near-black #0A0A0A grounds; rain-wet concrete and black-marble greys; one metallic accent per world —
  rose-gold #B76E79 (the House), gold #D4AF37 (Signature), silver #C0C0C0 (Black Rose), crimson #DC143C (Love Hurts).
Surface / backdrop: wet Oakland concrete, raw aggregate, black marble, port fog. Never a studio sweep.
Lighting baseline: one hard key or rim against deep shadow — luxury tier. Moonlight for Black Rose, warm predawn
  gold for Signature, controlled crimson bleed for Love Hurts, rose-gold dusk for Kids Capsule, rose-gold fissure
  light for the House.
Composition rule: rule of thirds; subject anchored in a lower or side third; a foreground depth element;
  one area of natural atmosphere (fog, night sky, blurred marble) kept tonally calm for caption overlay in post —
  part of the scene, never a flat band.
Style register: photographic luxury-streetwear editorial; cinematic anamorphic depth; civic-pride grit;
  monogram as protagonist; disciplined palette with one selective accent; sport DNA under the polish.
Contrast & saturation: bold tonal range, deep shadows, bright metallic highlights — louder than organic,
  never neon, never HDR halos.
Absolutely no blue or navy. No people's faces in round 1.
[AVOID — appended to every prompt]
no blue, no navy, no cyan, no cool-blue grade; no AI artifacts, no warped or smeared text, no fake words baked
into the image; no plastic or waxy surfaces, no cartoon rendering; no extra limbs or doubled subjects; no
oversaturated HDR, no HDR halos, no oversharpening; no flat fluorescent lighting, no on-camera flash; no stock
poses, no cliché compositions; no random logos, no watermarks, no invented marks or embroidery on the garment;
no warped garment lettering; no stale stock-photo aesthetic, no over-staged cleanliness, no obviously composited
backgrounds; no flat solid color bands, no empty rectangles, no dull gradient zones; no ornate baroque interior,
no chandeliers, no fairytale castle, no European luxury-house register; no pastel, no nursery, no toy cues.

QC gate — before anything advances a round

  1. Full-res only. Open the PNG the job returns. Never judge from the widget thumbnail or a contact sheet.
  2. Side-by-side against the reference , detail-for-detail: garment vs its Element source photo; monogram vs assets/branding/sr-monogram-rosegold-canonical.png ; Skyy vs assets/branding/mascot/skyy-canonical-reference.jpeg . "Looks like a plausible Black Rose hoodie" is a fail.
  3. No invented marks — extra embroidery, extra roses, changed lettering, a second logo — any one fails the render.
  4. No blue. Sample the shadows; a cool-blue grade fails.
  5. Caption area survives — the calm region reads as scene, not a band, and the safe zone is clear.
  6. Copy check — headline is canon-verbatim for its collection; CTA matches product state; no gendered framing; no urgency theatre.
  7. A previz that passes composition but fails garment detail still advances to round 2 — that's what the hero tier and the on-model reference are for. A previz that fails composition is re-prompted, not re-rolled.
