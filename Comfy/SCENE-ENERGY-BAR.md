# SkyyRose scene energy bar

This brief governs every remaining mapped commerce-scene execution. It does not
replace the scene ledger, SKU authority, approved environment, protected
customer/product layer, collection object, or founder approval gates.

## Mandatory campaign qualities

- The customer is a confident campaign protagonist, never a pasted catalog
  cutout.
- The exact mapped product is large, legible, desirable, and visually primary.
- The collection sculpture, monument, or story object has believable material
  weight, scale, support or attachment, perspective, depth, shadow, reflection
  or occlusion, and a deliberate relationship to the customer.
- Customer, product, brand object, and environment share one ground plane and
  one lighting system.
- Composition uses strong editorial hierarchy, intentional negative space,
  cinematic atmosphere, and professional restraint.
- Every visible element must advance product desire, customer identity,
  collection story, or commerce clarity.
- The shared visual grammar is cinematic urban grit: real material response,
  controlled imperfection, practical light, deep but readable blacks,
  atmospheric depth, and restrained film grain.
- Urban grit comes from rain-darkened concrete, weathered or blackened steel,
  fog, sodium-vapor warmth, cool Bay ambient light, skin texture, fabric
  texture, and believable wear. It is never a dirt overlay.
- Every contract must specify output geometry, subject boxes, horizon, contact
  points, lens class, camera height, key light, shadow behavior, floor response,
  reflection mode, and native-integration requirements before any paid
  execution.

## Automatic visual failures

- Cutout halo, pasted edge, floating foot, mismatched exposure, or incompatible
  perspective.
- Tiny or passive customer/product treatment.
- Wall-sticker logo or decorative brand object with no physical integration.
- Wrong garment, construction, colorway, patch, label, logo, wordmark, wearer,
  or SKU placement.
- Random spectacle, filler props, extra people, invented text, or
  cross-collection contamination.
- Technical completion without campaign presence.
- Generic hotel lobby, luxury apartment, anonymous showroom, random graffiti,
  neon cyberpunk, generic warehouse clutter, or grit-by-filter.
- Any sculpture or monument without supported weight, contact shadow, occlusion,
  perspective agreement, and the floor response required by the scene contract.

## Collection-specific expression

- **Signature:** West Coast industrial refinement, Golden Gate identity, marine
  fog, rose-gold craft, damp concrete, and confident customer presence.
- **Love Hurts:** romantic urban cinema, fractured civic architecture, red
  emergency light, controlled sensuality, enchanted drama, and exact product
  clarity.
- **Black Rose:** nocturnal Oakland gothic power, brutalist transit
  architecture, sodium-vapor practicals, controlled menace, and exact product
  authority.

## Credit discipline

- One scene contract can authorize at most one paid candidate and one paid
  attempt.
- Automatic paid retries are forbidden. Failure opens an issue and stops the
  loop.
- The current enhanced prompt must have a hash-bound independent PASS receipt;
  any prompt mutation invalidates the old receipt.
- Paid execution additionally requires a candidate-bound approval receipt tied
  to the current executable manifest fingerprint.
- Higgsfield creates candidate imagery and does not guarantee exact products.
  Comfy is correction-only after capture and may alter product pixels only
  inside an explicit mask using approved authority.
- A scene that already carries the target energy is preserved. SIG-COMMERCE-3
  receives only SG-001 and SG-003 mask-bound corrections—no full-frame
  regeneration spend.

External inspiration images remain inspiration only. They are never generation
inputs or source authority unless the founder explicitly changes their status.
