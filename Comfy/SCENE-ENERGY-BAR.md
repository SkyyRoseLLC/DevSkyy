# SkyyRose scene energy bar

This brief governs every remaining mapped commerce-scene execution. It does not replace the scene ledger, SKU authority, approved environment, protected customer/product layer, collection object, or founder approval gates.

## Mandatory campaign qualities

- The customer is a confident campaign protagonist, never a pasted catalog cutout.
- The exact mapped product is large, legible, desirable, and visually primary.
- The collection sculpture, monument, or story object has believable material weight, scale, support or attachment, perspective, depth, shadow, reflection or occlusion, and a deliberate relationship to the customer.
- Customer, product, brand object, and environment share one ground plane and one lighting system.
- Composition uses strong editorial hierarchy, intentional negative space, cinematic atmosphere, and professional restraint.
- Every visible element must advance product desire, customer identity, collection story, or commerce clarity.

## Automatic visual failures

- Cutout halo, pasted edge, floating foot, mismatched exposure, or incompatible perspective.
- Tiny or passive customer/product treatment.
- Wall-sticker logo or decorative brand object with no physical integration.
- Wrong garment, construction, colorway, patch, label, logo, wordmark, wearer, or SKU placement.
- Random spectacle, filler props, extra people, invented text, or cross-collection contamination.
- Technical completion without campaign presence.

## Collection-specific expression

- **Signature:** polished architectural luxury, Golden Gate identity, refined warmth, dimensional brand craft.
- **Love Hurts:** emotionally charged romantic cinema, controlled sensuality, enchanted drama, exact product clarity.
- **Black Rose:** nocturnal gothic power, urban monumentality, controlled menace, exact product authority.

External inspiration images remain inspiration only. They are never generation inputs or source authority unless the founder explicitly changes their status.
