# Cinematic shopping / fashion editorial direction

The user requests cinematic, inhabited fashion editorial scenes, not isolated cutouts on backdrops.

They are not replacements for locked model identities, garments, collection environments or product authority.

## Visual treatment
- Directional light motivated by the existing environment, with coherent exposure and color temperature across models.
- Grounded sole contact, believable cast shadows and restrained broken wet-floor reflections.
- Tactile fabric, lifelike skin, layered spatial atmosphere and controlled cinematic contrast.
- Keep clothing and artwork readable for shopping; avoid opaque fog, crushed blacks, generic color filters and decorative clutter.
- Preserve each collection world: Love Hurts cathedral; Black Rose moonlit Bay Bridge; Signature Golden Gate dusk.
- Signature SG-006 remains a long hoodie worn as a dress, with bare legs and no trousers.
