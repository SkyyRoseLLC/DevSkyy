# Cinematic urban-grit scene bible

This is the shared visual contract for the five remaining SkyyRose commerce
scenes. It converts the founder's reference energy into original
collection-specific direction; the reference image is inspiration only and is
not a source or generation input.

## Shared visual grammar

The customer and exact product are the campaign. Faces, stance, silhouette,
fabric construction, artwork placement, and product readability come before
architecture and spectacle. Sculpture placement is flexible only when it feels
inevitable: freestanding or architecturally attached, correctly scaled,
perspective-matched, visibly supported, and sharing contact, shadow, reflection,
occlusion, depth, and grain with the scene.

Cinematic urban grit is physical rather than decorative: rain-darkened concrete,
blackened or weathered steel, marine fog, practical sodium-vapor warmth against
cool ambient sky, deep but readable blacks, controlled imperfection, skin
texture, fabric texture, and restrained film grain. Do not use generic
showrooms, hotel lobbies, luxury apartments, random graffiti, neon cyberpunk,
warehouse prop piles, dirt overlays, pasted people, sticker logos, or decorative
sculpture pockets.

## OODA and spend gates

1. **Observe:** verify catalog, image SOT, every source hash, authority
   dependency, deny-list, candidate identity, and prior rejection.
2. **Orient:** apply the scene's creative direction and measured optical
   contract; classify protected pixels and the provider's actual capability.
3. **Decide:** independently review the current enhanced prompt. Any prompt
   mutation invalidates the prior review receipt. Authorize exactly one
   candidate against the current executable manifest fingerprint.
4. **Act:** submit no more than one paid candidate. Hash it immediately. Route
   it to local Comfy only for defect-classified, protected corrections. A
   failure opens an issue and stops; it does not retry.
5. **Decide again:** a different independent reviewer scores product fidelity,
   optical realism, physical integration, story, and commerce hierarchy. Only
   the founder can promote a surviving candidate.

Provider success, local Comfy success, a passing test, or a release-evidence
PASS means candidate readiness only. None authorizes SOT replacement, theme
wiring, deployment, or visual promotion.
