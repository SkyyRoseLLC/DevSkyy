# Fashion Theme Team inside the scene OODA

The installed `fashion-theme-team@personal` control plane is embedded in every
remaining scene contract through `scene-contracts/scene-ooda-index.json`. The
team governs authority, creative direction, execution, independent review, and
release evidence; it does not replace the scene ledger, product SOT, or founder
approval.

## Role routing

| OODA stage       | Fashion Theme Team owner                                           | Required handoff                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Observe          | `catalog-sot-integrator` under `fashion-theme-lead`                | Current catalog, image SOT, source hashes, prior decisions, blockers, and candidate identity                                                |
| Orient           | `brand-experience-architect` using `product-fidelity-image-edits`  | Cinematic urban-grit direction, exact-product constraints, protected regions, measured optical contract, composition, and prompt assertions |
| Act: capture     | Explicitly approved provider runtime using `elite-builder-runtime` | One bounded Higgsfield `hero_banner` candidate and immutable provider receipt                                                               |
| Act: correction  | `product-fidelity-image-edits` capability                          | Comfy staging and only mask-bounded or authority-preserving corrections                                                                     |
| Decide           | Fresh independent `visual-commerce-qa`                             | Separate scores for product fidelity, physical integration, optical realism, collection story, and commerce hierarchy                       |
| Release evidence | `theme-release-engineer`                                           | Candidate hash, complete evidence, rollback/quarantine status, and `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `BLOCKED`                                             |
| Promotion        | Founder                                                            | Explicit `FOUNDER_APPROVED_VISUAL`; never inferred from any team or provider pass                                                           |

## Non-negotiable loop behavior

- A rejected image is evidence only. It cannot become a generation reference,
  scene authority, product authority, or wireable asset.
- Every mutation creates a new candidate identity and invalidates review
  evidence from the prior candidate.
- A blocked scene does not prevent source-complete scenes from continuing
  through no-spend validation or enhancement.
- Provider execution is a separately approved paid action. Enhancement-only
  calls do not authorize generation.
- Every paid route is fail-closed behind `ONE_CANDIDATE_ONE_PAID_ATTEMPT`, a
  current prompt-review receipt, and a candidate-bound approval receipt. There
  is no automatic paid retry.
- Comfy staging does not authorize runtime wiring, theme promotion, deployment,
  or SOT replacement.
- Comfy is post-capture protected correction only. Product-pixel changes require
  an explicit mask and approved physical authority; full-frame product redraw is
  forbidden.
- Higgsfield is a candidate/reference provider, not an exact-product guarantee.
  A successful provider response never closes product fidelity or
  native-integration review.
- The independent reviewer checks the measured optical contract: geometry,
  horizon, contact, lens, light, shadow, floor, reflection, grain, depth,
  occlusion, and edge spill.
- `visual-commerce-qa` remains independent and does not edit the candidate it
  reviews.
- `theme-release-engineer` may issue readiness evidence but may not deploy or
  waive a failed gate.
