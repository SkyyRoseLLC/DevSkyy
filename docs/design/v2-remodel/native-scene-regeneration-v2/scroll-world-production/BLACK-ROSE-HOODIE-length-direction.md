# BLACK Rose Hoodie (`br-004`) length direction

**Founder styling direction — 2026-08-27.** The regular `br-004` **BLACK Rose
Hoodie** is a long pullover hoodie, not a standard short hoodie.

- **Women-led authority and scenes:** wear the same physical hoodie as a
  hoodie dress, with its original hem reading around mid-thigh. Show bare legs
  rather than adding a skirt, dress overlay, or unverified lower garment.
- **Men-led authority and scenes:** keep the original hoodie as a longer,
  seat-covering pullover over trousers. Do not turn it into a dress.
- Preserve `br-004`'s physical hoodie construction: black body/hood, tonal
  black drawstrings, kangaroo pocket, black ribbing, and one large centered
  monochrome rose/cloud embroidery above the pouch. The regular BR-004 has no
  side-body embroidery. Do not add a second crest, text, patches, or new
  decorations.

This is a visual-styling rule for source-stage and scene candidates. It does
not merge or override the distinct `br-005` BLACK Rose Hoodie — Signature
Edition: right-chest silicone-cut logo, sublimated-rose hood lining, and
side-body embroidery are exclusive to BR-005.
