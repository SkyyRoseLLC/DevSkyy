## Visual locks checked before generation

- White perforated mesh basketball-short body with a solid black waistband.
- Black perforated side panels; black horizontal pocket welts; black lower
  binding; red-and-white perimeter piping.
- Repeating orange-rose, green-stem, white-cloud art on the white mesh.
- The cursive Love Hurts marks are physical artwork, not freely regenerated
  copy. The right-front mark and side-panel lettering must only be retained if

## Script and composition lock

Render one full-body woman in a neutral warm-grey studio on a real floor plane,
wearing a plain fitted black short-sleeve tee, black crew socks, and black
trainers. Camera is straight-on, fashion-editorial but not catalog-cutout;
shorts fully visible from waistband to hem with both side panels unobscured.
The asset must be a front three-quarter that preserves the photographed
right-front cursive mark and the left/right side-panel construction. Do not
ask the model to manufacture wording: when the mark cannot resolve faithfully,
the candidate fails visual QA and remains quarantined.
