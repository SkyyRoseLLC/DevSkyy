## Detail and script locks checked before generation

- **Founder styling direction (2026-08-27):** on women, the physical long
  hoodie is worn and visualized as a hoodie dress: the existing hoodie hem
  reaches approximately mid-thigh and is worn with bare legs. This is not a
  separate dress, skirt, added hem panel, or altered pocket construction. On
  men, the same physical hoodie must read as a longer, seat-covering hoodie
  worn over trousers. This styling direction also applies to the distinct
  `br-004` BLACK Rose Hoodie; it does not alter `br-005` Signature Edition.
- A solid mint cotton-fleece **pullover hoodie**: no zipper, no crewneck
  collar, no white body, no color blocking, no rainbow chevron, no pink hood.
- Solid mint hood and sleeves, white drawstrings, full-width self-color mint
  kangaroo pouch, and self-color ribbed cuffs and hem.
- One large centered lavender/purple rose cluster with white highlights,
  grey-green thorny stems/leaves, and a grey-white cloud base above the pouch.
- No chest wordmark, slogan, signature, decorative lettering, sleeve art,
  patch, bridge print, or back artwork. The artwork is the only front mark.
- The candidate must show the hood, two drawstrings, chest art, pouch, and
  full body on a real studio floor plane. Plain black or dark charcoal bottoms
  may be used to keep the garment legible; no competing branded item or prop.
